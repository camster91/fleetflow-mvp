# Fleetvera brand assets

Production-ready identity assets for Fleetvera. The master usage rules, palette, voice, accessibility guidance, and rollout notes live in `docs/brand/FLEETVERA_BRAND_GUIDE.md`.

## Included

- `logo/`: horizontal, reversed, icon, and wordmark SVG masters
- `icons/`: favicon, Apple touch icon, and 192/512 px PWA icons
- `og-image.png`: 1200 × 630 social sharing card
- `twitter-card.png`: 1200 × 630 social sharing card

Use `logo-horizontal.svg` on light surfaces and `logo-horizontal-dark.svg` on evergreen or dark surfaces. Use the icon at compact sizes. Always use the accessible name “Fleetvera”.

Regenerate raster icons after changing the SVG master with `node scripts/generate-icons.js`.
