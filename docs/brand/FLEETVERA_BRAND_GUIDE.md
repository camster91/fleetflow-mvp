# Fleetvera brand guide

## Brand foundation

**Name:** Fleetvera  
**Tagline:** Fleet operations, organized.  
**Positioning:** A calm, practical operations workspace for teams coordinating vehicles, deliveries, maintenance, clients, and reporting.  
**Personality:** capable, clear, dependable, quietly modern.  
**Voice:** direct and useful. Prefer concrete verbs and plain language. Avoid hype, jargon, and claims the product cannot prove.

## Logo system

The route-shaped V represents two locations converging into one organized path. The two endpoints also suggest coordinated teams and fleet movement.

- `logo-horizontal.svg`: primary logo on light backgrounds
- `logo-horizontal-dark.svg`: reversed logo on evergreen or dark photography
- `logo-icon.svg`: app icon, avatar, favicon source, and compact navigation
- `logo-wordmark.svg`: wordmark where the icon is already present

Keep clear space equal to half the icon height on every side. Do not rotate, stretch, recolor, outline, or add effects to the logo. The horizontal logo must not appear below 132 px wide; use the icon below that size.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Evergreen | `#123C36` | Navigation, primary actions, headings |
| Dark | Deep Evergreen | `#082B27` | Footer, high-emphasis surfaces |
| Signal | Route Teal | `#42D6A4` | Focus, active states, data accents |
| Accent | Dispatch Lime | `#B8F36B` | Logo route, sparing highlights |
| Canvas | Fleet Mist | `#F4F8F7` | Page backgrounds |
| Text | Operations Ink | `#102421` | Primary copy |
| Muted | Slate Green | `#58706B` | Secondary copy |

Evergreen with white is the default accessible button pairing. Lime is an accent, not a body-text color on white.

## Typography

Use **Inter** for product and marketing interfaces, with the system sans-serif stack as fallback. Use 700 weight for display headings, 600 for controls and subheads, and 400–500 for body copy. Use tabular numerals for metrics where supported.

## UI principles

1. Operational clarity wins over decoration.
2. One clear primary action per view.
3. Status never relies on color alone.
4. Controls remain at least 44 px tall on touch screens.
5. Dense data may scroll inside its own region, but must never widen the page.
6. Use 12–16 px radii and restrained evergreen-tinted shadows.

## Messaging

**Primary headline:** Fleet operations, organized.  
**Short description:** Keep vehicles, deliveries, maintenance, clients, reports, and team access together in one dependable workspace.  
**CTA:** Sign in  
**Invitation note:** New workspaces are currently created by invitation.

Do not advertise live GPS, automated optimization, or integrations unless those capabilities are verified in the released product.

## Domain and rollout

`fleetvera.com` and `fleetvera.ca` were research candidates with no RDAP registration record on 2026-08-07. That is not a purchase guarantee. Register and verify a domain before changing production email, canonical URLs, DNS, or public contact addresses. Internal database names and the legacy session cookie may remain unchanged during the first brand rollout to protect continuity.
